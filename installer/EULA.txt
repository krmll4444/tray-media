PRIVACY SUMMARY
================================================================================

Tray Media is a local Windows utility. It does not require an account and does
not include analytics, advertising, or telemetry SDKs.

On your device it may store:
- Settings in %LocalAppData%\TrayMedia\settings.json
- An optional autostart entry under HKCU\...\Run
- Optional tray pin preferences in Windows notification icon settings

It may read Windows System Media Transport Controls (Now Playing) and theme
colors so the popover can control media already playing on your PC. That
information stays on your device.
